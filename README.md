# Frogtown Skills Hub

A skill-sharing directory for the Frogtown neighborhood in Saint Paul, MN — built by and for the neighborhood, not a startup product.

The idea is simple: neighbors have skills, tools, and knowledge worth sharing, and there wasn't a good way to find each other. So this is that. You offer what you can help with, a community admin reviews it before it goes public, and then anyone in the neighborhood can search the directory and reach out.

## What's actually in it

**The directory.** Browse or search neighbors by name, skill, or language. Everything's organized by quadrant (Frogtown is split into thirteen of them) so you can find help nearby without anyone having to share an exact address — just a general area and maybe cross streets.

**Offering your skills.** A short form: who you are, what you can help with, roughly where you are, what languages you speak. Submissions sit in a review queue until an admin approves them — nothing goes live automatically.

**Messaging.** Once you find someone, you can message them right on the site. Realtime, so replies show up without refreshing. There's a report button on every conversation in case something feels off.

**Neighborhood updates.** A small news feed on the homepage for notices, events, and general updates from the admin team.

**Admin panel.** Where submissions get reviewed and approved, listings get managed, reports get looked at, and the news feed gets posted to. Also where the site's own settings live — the homepage hero text, the "who runs this" list, the contact email — so none of that is hardcoded.

## Why it looks the way it does

No payments, no marketplace, no algorithm deciding what you see. Just a trusted list of neighbors, reviewed by real people, organized by where you actually live. That's the whole pitch.
